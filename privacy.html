Privacy.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Using this website

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate visits, page views, referring websites and page performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage to track visitors. We do not collect reflection-sheet answers through analytics. This website does not use advertising pixels or a newsletter signup form. Our hosting and domain providers, Render and Cloudflare, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the website.

Learn more about Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Your reflection sheet

Answers entered into the reflection sheet stay in your current browser page. This website does not transmit or save them. Refreshing or closing the page clears them. Printing or saving a PDF uses your browser.

Contact and external links

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your enquiry. Links to Amazon, Pinterest and LinkedIn take you to those services, whose privacy policies apply there. Book links may include Amazon Attribution parameters so Amazon can report aggregate clicks and attributed book purchases. Those links do not contain your worksheet answers. Amazon processes activity on its services under its own privacy notice.
