01 / THE IDEA

Every difficult
conversation has
three chairs.

01 One belongs to you.

02 One belongs to the other person.

03 The third belongs to the conversation itself.

The Third Chair asks you to step far enough beyond your position to see what the conversation actually needs. Through candid stories, practical reflection and six decisions, Dragan Andric explores the conversations we avoid at work, in relationships and throughout life.

You cannot control how another person responds. You can consider how you want to show up.

02 / WHERE IT MEETS YOU

The room changes.
The conversation matters.

I.

At work.

The feedback you owe someone. The boundary you need to set. The question nobody wants to ask.

II.

In relationships.

The tension beneath “I’m fine.” The need you have not named. The difference you are trying to understand.

III.

With yourself.

The story you keep repeating. The assumption worth questioning. The part that is yours to own.
